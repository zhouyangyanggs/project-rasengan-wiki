## Card types and deck structure

If you searched for Naruto card game 2027, start with the confirmed facts below. A NARUTO CARD GAME deck contains 51 main cards, 5 Chakra cards, and 1 Summon card. The deck is built around a Leader Card, and victory requires reducing the opposing Leader's Life to 0. Character Cards are chosen according to the Leader's color and form your front line.

EX Character Cards are stronger units that can only enter play when specific conditions are met. Chakra Cards pay for Ninjutsu and other support effects. Summon Cards are required to deploy Character Cards to the field. All five card types are described on the official site.

## The core battle loop

The official tagline is "Read your rival, Unleash Ninjutsu!" The loop that phrase describes is central: you pay Chakra to trigger Ninjutsu that disrupts your opponent's plans. Reading the opponent's board and resources is therefore the core skill.

Because Ninjutsu depends on Chakra, timing matters as much as card power. Deciding when to place cards as Chakra and when to keep them in hand creates the game's main decisions. The official site presents this as the identity of the game.
